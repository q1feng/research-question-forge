# Changelog

## v1.0

- Turn observations and early ideas into research questions, feasible directions, and a revisable research framework.
- Use literature, baseline comparisons, and new feedback to revise question abstraction, expansion, decomposition, and framing, while reflecting on how observations and questions are formed.
- Support researcher-led decisions, composable workflows, and bilingual use through either an installed Skill or a standalone guide.

### 中文说明

- 帮助用户从观察与初步想法形成研究问题、可开展的方向和可持续修订的研究框架。
- 通过文献、基线比较与新反馈调整问题抽象、拓展、拆分及框架，并反思观察方式与问题形成的前提。
- 保留研究者决定权，支持组合其他工作流，提供中英文 Skill 与免安装引导。
